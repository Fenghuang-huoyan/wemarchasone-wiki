This Privacy Policy describes **wemarchasone.wiki** only. It is not dux’s privacy policy, and it is not Valve or Steam’s.

## 1. What the Site Stores

The site is compiled as static HTML, CSS, and client-side calculator scripts. It does not create user accounts, does not collect passwords, and never asks for your Steam login. All unit counts and perk selections in the Army & Wave Calculator run strictly in browser memory.

## 2. Analytics & Cookies (`GA4`)

`G-SDWYYDDBP3` is configured in `sites/wemarchasone/site.config.ts` and loaded via `googletagmanager.com/gtag/js`. Google Analytics sets first-party cookies (`_ga` and `_ga_*`) in your browser to distinguish anonymous visits and measure aggregated page views. You can block cookies in your browser settings or install the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

## 3. Advertising Status

Display advertising in `sites/wemarchasone/ads.config.ts` is **disabled** (`enabled: false`). No `bauval.org` or third-party ad network scripts load on the homepage, guide pages, or 404 page.

## 4. Outbound Links & Contact

Links to the Steam store (`store.steampowered.com`) open external pages governed by Valve’s own privacy policy. For editorial corrections or privacy questions regarding **wemarchasone.wiki**, email `malee05050264@gmail.com`. (The developer support address `playwemarchasone@gmail.com` printed on Steam reaches the game developer, not this fan wiki.)
