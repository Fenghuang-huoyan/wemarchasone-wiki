## How the Economy & Survival Math Works in Code

From `ShapeWar.EconomyTuning` inside `resources.assets`:

- **Uncleared Defeat / Victory Kill Gold (`unclearedKillGold = 0.40`)**: On any stage you have not cleared yet, every enemy shape you destroy pays **40% of its `goldValue`** (`0.4g` per Circle, `1.2g` per Square/Triangle, `2.0g` per Diamond, `120g` per Pentagon/Hexagon/Kite, `400g` per Chevron), win or lose.
- **Replay Kill Gold (`killGold = 0.20`)**: Replaying an already cleared level pays **20%** of defeated enemy unit values (`repeatReward = 0.10`).
- **Star Multipliers (`markHeld = 0.50`, `markMastered = 0.50`)**: Earning **DECISIVE** adds `+50%` bonus Gold, and **MASTERED** (3 stars) adds another `+50%` (further boosted by `+20%` once you unlock the **Mastery** node for `260g`).
