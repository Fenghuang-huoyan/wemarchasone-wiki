<Callout kind="note" title="Why Chevrons Delete Enemy Hexagons (1.80x Effective Multiplier)">
Enemy **Hexagons** shrug off 70% of arrow and cavalry damage (`0.30x`), making basic swarms stall against them from Level 16 onward. However, Hexagons have `armorBolt = 1.20` (+20% bolt vulnerability) while **Chevrons** carry `vsBigDamage = 1.50`—combining for **720 effective damage per bolt** before piercing!
</Callout>

## Command Mode Controls & Crown Mechanics (Verified in `Battle_en` & `Help_en`)

- **Crowning The King (`160g` Skill Node)**: Once unlocked on the Circle branch, **double-click (`LMB ×2`) any Circle** during pre-battle placement to crown it as your King (**10x HP = 100 HP, 10x Damage = 40 Dmg**, and **+30% move & attack speed** to nearby allies; upgradable with *Command Voice* for 2x aura radius and *King's Armor* for 90% damage reduction).
- **Real-Time Tactical Orders (`RtsOrderKind`)**:
  - **Attack Move (`RMB`)**: March to target point, engage enemies along the path, or focus-fire a clicked enemy.
  - **Move (`E`)**: Reposition or retreat to the cursor without stopping to fight.
  - **Hold (`R`)**: Stop in place and brace against incoming charges while attacking enemies in reach.
  - **Release Orders (`T`) / Select Army (`Q`)**: Clear manual orders or select your entire fielded force.
