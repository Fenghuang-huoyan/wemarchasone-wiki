In **We March as One**, your army is composed entirely of geometric shapes rather than conventional military or fantasy classes. Following the official Steam launch on November 13, 2026, the complete roster and troop placement rules are established: players start with Circles and expand their recruitment roster through the branching skill tree.

For how to unlock these shapes and scale army capacity, see [Progression](/progression). For overview gameplay, see the [guide](/guide).

## The Seven Geometric Unit Shapes

The game features seven distinct geometric units available across Campaign, Tactics, Command, Muster, and Sandbox modes. Instead of text stat menus, each shape's combat function is defined by its mass, geometry, and tactical archetype.

## Painting Units onto the Battlefield

Units are positioned before battle using real-time canvas tools rather than grid snap slots:

- **Brush Tool & Size Control**: Players select a unit shape and use adjustable brush controls to paint formations directly onto their half of the battlefield.
- **Formation Controls**: The preparation UI provides **Undo**, **Clear**, **Upgrades**, **Levels**, and **Fight** buttons.
- **Pre-Battle Tactical Orders**:
  - **MARCH**: Issues an advance directive to move forward, push into gaps, or execute flanking maneuvers.
  - **HOLD**: Instructs troops to maintain current positioning, brace defensively, and hold bridges or choke points against incoming enemy charges.

## Combined Arms & Deployment Constraints

The campaign encourages mixing shapes to counter distinct enemy force compositions:

- **Single Unit Victory**: The achievement **One of a Kind** ("Win level 10 or later with a single unit type") proves that mono-shape armies can win early-to-mid levels through pure numbers or positioning.
- **Combined Arms**: The achievement **Combined Arms** ("Win a campaign battle with five or more unit types on the field") highlights the mid-to-late game tactical requirement of blending frontline shields (Squares), line infantry (Circles), shock attackers (Triangles), and specialized shapes.
- **The King Commander**: Through the skill tree, crowning a Circle transforms it into "The King", a commanding unit with increased durability and a combat aura that enhances adjacent shapes.

## Individually Simulated Physics vs. Hidden Numbers

The store description emphasizes that *"every soldier and projectile is individually simulated"*. In practice:

- Combat is resolved via physical mass, collision momentum, projectile arcs, and line-of-sight interactions rather than turn-based dice rolls or spreadsheet stat trading.
- During battle, players can toggle between **2D**, **3D**, and **fly-through** camera modes, alongside simulation speed toggles (**1x**, **2x**, **4x**).

<Callout kind="warn" title="Unverified Stat Blocks">
The game does not display numeric character sheets (such as numerical Hit Points, DPS values, or armor ratings). Any third-party tier list claiming exact numeric base stats for Circles or Squares is unverified (未核实). Battlefield efficacy depends on paint density, terrain choke points, and command orders.
</Callout>
