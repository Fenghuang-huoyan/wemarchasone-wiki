In **We March as One**, your army is composed entirely of geometric shapes rather than conventional military classes. According to the official Steam store text and Steam achievements, players start with shapes, paint troops onto the battlefield, and expand their army with new unit types unlocked through a branching skill tree.

For how to unlock new unit types and scale army capacity, see [Progression](/progression). For overview gameplay, see the [guide](/guide).

## The Seven Geometric Unit Shapes

The game client features seven distinct geometric units available across game modes. Instead of text stat menus, each shape's combat function is defined by its mass, geometry, and tactical archetype. Because the store description and achievement lists do not enumerate shape names or combat roles, specific unit profiles reflect client observations rather than official published documentation.

<Callout kind="note" title="Unit Unlocks Confirmed by Steam Achievements">
Official Steam achievements confirm unlocking unit types: **Fresh Recruits** requires you to *"Unlock a second unit type"*, **Combined Arms** requires winning a battle with *"five or more unit types on the field"*, and **Full Roster** requires you to *"Unlock every unit type"*. The specific count of seven geometric shapes and their individual names are observed in the game client, but are not enumerated in the achievement text or store description.

## Painting Units onto the Battlefield

Units are positioned before battle using real-time canvas tools rather than grid snap slots:

- **Brush Tool & Size Control**: The Steam store description states: *"Paint your troops onto the battlefield however you like, and you position them to counter the enemy force."* Players paint formations directly using adjustable brush controls.
- **Formation Controls**: The preparation UI provides **Undo**, **Clear**, **Upgrades**, **Levels**, and **Fight** buttons.
- **Pre-Battle Tactical Orders (Unverified / ❓)**:
  - **MARCH**: Issues an advance directive to move forward, push into gaps, or execute flanking maneuvers.
  - **HOLD**: Instructs troops to maintain current positioning, brace defensively, and hold bridges or choke points against incoming enemy charges.

## Combined Arms & Deployment Constraints

The campaign encourages mixing shapes to counter distinct enemy force compositions:

- **Single Unit Victory**: The achievement **One of a Kind** ("Win level 10 or later using a single unit type") proves that mono-shape armies can win early-to-mid levels through pure numbers or positioning.
- **Combined Arms**: The achievement **Combined Arms** ("Win a campaign battle with five or more unit types on the field") confirms that at least five distinct unit types can be deployed together.
- **The King Commander (Unverified / ❓)**: In-game client skill trees allow crowning a Circle unit as "The King". This commander unit is an in-game feature not mentioned in official store copy or Steam achievements.

## Individually Simulated Physics vs. Hidden Numbers

The store description emphasizes that *"every soldier and projectile is individually simulated"*. In practice:

- Combat is resolved via physical mass, collision momentum, projectile arcs, and line-of-sight interactions rather than turn-based dice rolls or spreadsheet stat trading.
- During battle, players can toggle between camera modes (isometric, top-down, or fly-through), alongside simulation speed toggles.

<Callout kind="warn" title="Unverified Stat Blocks">
The game does not display numeric character sheets (such as numerical Hit Points, DPS values, or armor ratings). Any third-party tier list claiming exact numeric base stats for Circles or Squares is unverified (未核实). Battlefield efficacy depends on paint density, terrain choke points, and command orders.
</Callout>
