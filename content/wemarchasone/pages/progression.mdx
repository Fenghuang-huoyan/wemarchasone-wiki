Progression in **We March as One** ties battle outcomes directly to army growth. The core loop is verified by official Steam store text and Steam achievements: players push through a 50-level campaign (*We March as One* achievement), earn gold on win or loss (*Lessons Learned*), reinvest into a branching skill tree (*Every Last Leaf*), and expand army capacity (*Full Muster*).

For individual troop shapes and placement rules, see [Units](/units). For overall game systems, see the [guide](/guide).

## The Core Loop: Gold, Army Persistence, and Defeat

The campaign economy is defined by persistent progression rules:

1. **Gold on Win or Lose**: Defeated enemy shapes yield gold regardless of battle outcome. The store page states you earn gold whether you win or lose, and the achievement **Lessons Learned** ("Lose a battle") acknowledges defeat as part of normal progression.
2. **Persistent Army Size**: Fallen friendly troops return for the next battle. As described on Steam: *"Fallen troops return for the next battle, so you keep the army you have built."*
3. **No Respecs (Unverified / ❓)**: In-game client purchases on the campaign skill tree are permanent without an in-game respec button; this restriction is not published in store documentation.

<Callout kind="note" title="A Loss is Still Economic Progress">
Because gold is credited from enemies defeated during the engagement, repeated attempts against difficult hordes steadily accumulate gold to purchase army capacity or new troop types.
</Callout>

The campaign features 50 sequential levels. Every ten levels culminates in a **City** encounter verified by the *Sacked* achievement:

### Victory Badges: Decisive and Heroic

Levels can be cleared with performance ratings:
- **DECISIVE**: Awarded for swift victories (*Decisive* achievement: "Earn a DECISIVE rating on any level").
- **HEROIC**: Awarded for high-efficiency survival (*Heroic* achievement: "Earn a HEROIC rating on any level").
- **Mastered**: Awarded for holding both ratings on the same stage (*Mastered* achievement: "Hold both DECISIVE and HEROIC ratings on the same level").
- **Campaign Mastery Tiers**: Sequential achievements track holding both DECISIVE and HEROIC ratings across 10-level campaign tiers: *Man-at-Arms* (levels 1–10), *Knight* (levels 11–20), *Captain* (levels 21–30), *Marshal* (levels 31–40), *Grand Marshal* (levels 41–50), culminating in *Conqueror* (all 50 levels).

## Game Modes & Sandbox Progression

Outside the linear 50-level Campaign, the game features distinct modes confirmed by Steam achievements and store descriptions:

- **Tactics Mode**: Tactical puzzle battles confirmed by the achievement **Tactician** ("Solve a Tactics board puzzle").
- **Command Mode**: Direct battle engagements confirmed by the achievement **Hands On** ("Win a Command battle").
- **Muster Mode (Unverified / ❓)**: Draft-style budget battles. Mentioned in community gameplay, but unreferenced in Steam store text or official achievement descriptions.
- **Sandbox Mode & Level Editor**: Confirmed by the Steam store description and the achievement **Blank Canvas** ("Start a battle in the Sandbox").
  - *Unlock Rules (Unverified / ❓)*: The mechanism where Sandbox elements unlock as they are encountered across game modes is observed in-game client behavior and is not documented in public store copy.
