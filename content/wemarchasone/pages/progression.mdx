Progression in **We March as One** combines persistent troop ownership with a deterministic Gold bounty formula. Below are the exact economy multipliers and skill-tree node prices unpacked from **Build `#26192930`** (`EconomyTuning` + `DemoSkillTree`). To simulate how these upgrades change your wave clear odds, open the **[Army & Wave Calculator](/calculator)**.

## Exact Gold Economy Formulas (`ShapeWar.EconomyTuning`)

<div class="table-scroll">

| Economy Parameter | Unpacked Value | How It Works in Campaign |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `startingGold` | **0g** | You begin Level 1 with **5 free Circles** (`rosterCircles = 5`) against 5 enemy Circles (`reward = 30g`). |
| `unclearedKillGold` | **0.40 (40%)** | On any uncleared stage, every enemy killed pays **40% of its `goldValue`** even if your army is wiped out. |
| `killGold` / `repeatReward` | **0.20 / 0.10** | Replaying an already-cleared stage pays reduced kill bounties after all stars are claimed. |
| `markHeld` (**DECISIVE**) | **+0.50 (+50%)** | Bonus Gold multiplier for earning the DECISIVE rating. |
| `markMastered` (**MASTERED**) | **+0.50 (+50%)** | Additional Gold multiplier for earning full 3-star mastery on a stage. |

</div>
