Progression in **We March as One** ties battle outcomes directly to army growth. Following the November 13, 2026 Steam release, the progression mechanics are verified: players push through a 50-level campaign, reinvest gold into a branching node graph, and expand recruitment capacity toward tens of thousands of individually simulated shapes.

For individual troop shapes and placement rules, see [Units](/units). For overall game systems, see the [guide](/guide).

## The Core Loop: Gold, Army Persistence, and Defeat

The campaign economy is defined by three persistent rules:

1. **Gold on Win or Lose**: Defeated enemy shapes yield gold regardless of battle outcome. Even a lost battle contributes economic resources toward the next upgrade. The achievement **Lessons Learned** ("Lose a battle") acknowledges defeat as a natural progression step.
2. **Persistent Army Size**: Fallen friendly troops return for the next battle. Losses are not permanent wipes; your army size accumulates as you recruit more shapes.
3. **No Respecs**: Node purchases on the campaign skill tree are permanent. Strategy comes from prioritizing capacity versus specialized branch upgrades.

<Callout kind="note" title="A Loss is Still Economic Progress">
Because gold is credited from enemies defeated during the engagement, repeated attempts against difficult hordes steadily accumulate gold to purchase army capacity or new troop types.
</Callout>

## The Branching Skill Tree Architecture

Accessible via the **Upgrades** button on the preparation screen, the skill tree is structured as an interconnected node graph. Upgrades divide into four primary tracks:

<div class="table-scroll">

| Upgrade Track | In-Game Function | Strategic Role | Verified Milestone / Achievement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Unit Type Unlocks** | Unlocks the six additional shapes (Square, Triangle, Hexagon, Pentagon, Diamond, Kite) | Expands tactical palette beyond starting Circles | *Fresh Recruits* (2nd unit), *Full Roster* (all 7 units) |
| **Army Capacity** | Increases maximum soldier count placed onto the battlefield | Essential for scaling army numbers toward tens of thousands | *Full Muster* (unlock all units + buy all army capacity upgrades) |
| **The King Branch** | Crowns a Circle unit into "The King" commander | Provides a powerful stat aura and defensive boost to surrounding formations | Launch client skill tree node |
| **Shape Specializations** | Multipliers to specific shape performance (durability, pierce, spacing) | Specializes the army around chosen compositions | Verified branch nodes |
| **Tree Mastery** | Overall node progression across the graph | Tracks total upgrades unlocked across the campaign | *Studied* (50% of tree), *Every Last Leaf* (100% of tree) |

</div>

## 50-Level Campaign & City Fortress Milestones

The campaign features 50 sequential levels. Every ten levels culminates in a fortified **City** encounter requiring strategic composition and flanking orders:

<div class="table-scroll">

| Campaign Stage | Level Range | Key Encounter / Milestone | Verified Achievement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **The Opening March** | Levels 1–5 | Tutorial skirmishes; basic Circle swarms | *The March Begins* (Win battle 1), *First Steps* (Win level 5) |
| **First City Siege** | Levels 6–10 | Level 10 City fortress; choke point battles | *Key to the City* (Win level 10), *Sacked* (Decisive on level 10) |
| **The Advance** | Levels 11–20 | Level 20 City fortress; multi-unit enemy hordes | *No Turning Back* (Win level 20), *Sacked* (Decisive on level 20) |
| **The Midpoint** | Levels 21–30 | Level 30 City fortress; river/building obstacles | *Over the Hill* (Win level 30), *Sacked* (Decisive on level 30) |
| **The Long March** | Levels 31–40 | Level 40 City fortress; dense mass formations | *The Long March* (Win level 40), *Sacked* (Decisive on level 40) |
| **The Grand Finale** | Levels 41–50 | Level 50 Imperial City; full-scale army clashes | *We March as One* (Win level 50), *Conqueror* (Decisive & Heroic on all 50) |

</div>

### Victory Badges: Decisive and Heroic

Levels can be cleared with performance ratings:
- **DECISIVE**: Awarded for swift, overwhelming victories (*Decisive* achievement).
- **HEROIC**: Awarded for high-efficiency survival or challenging constraints (*Heroic* achievement).
- **Mastered**: Awarded for securing both DECISIVE and HEROIC ratings on the same stage (*Mastered* achievement; ranks scale from *Man-at-Arms* through *Grand Marshal* and *Conqueror*).

## Game Modes & Sandbox Progression

Outside the linear 50-level Campaign, the game offers specialized game modes that share unlock mechanics:

- **Tactics Mode**: Fixed-army puzzles where troop placement and pre-battle MARCH/HOLD orders are the sole win condition (*Tactician* achievement).
- **Command Mode**: Real-time tactical engagements featuring direct battlefield orders and abilities during active combat (*Hands On* achievement).
- **Muster Mode**: Budget-limited puzzles where players select their own unit compositions within strict coin limits.
- **Sandbox Mode & Level Editor**: Full level editor where players build custom maps and stage battles (*Blank Canvas* achievement). **Unlock Rule**: Troops and upgrades are unlocked in the Sandbox as they are encountered across Campaign, Tactics, Command, and Muster modes.
